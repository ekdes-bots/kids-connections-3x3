# 🧩 Kids Connections Daily

A lightweight, daily 3x3 word-grouping puzzle game inspired by NYT Connections, tailored for kids and tweens (ages 10–13). 

## Features
- **Daily Rotation:** A new 3x3 puzzle unlocks every day at 7:00 AM ET.
- **75+ Puzzles:** Built-in library of tween-friendly science, history, geometry, and pop culture puzzles.
- **Streak & History Tracker:** Automatically saves winning streaks and past puzzle results using local storage.
- **Mobile Optimized:** Fully responsive touch support designed for smooth play on iPhones and browsers.

## How to Play
1. Review the 9 words on the grid.
2. Select 3 words that share a common theme.
3. Tap **Submit** to check your guess. You have 4 mistakes allowed!
